## Rules

- Report only verified progress.
- Do not mark incomplete work as finished.
- Separate blockers from risks.
- Include failed or unavailable tests.
- Do not invent project-completion percentages; explain the calculation.
- Do not execute Git commands.
- Do not commit or push.
